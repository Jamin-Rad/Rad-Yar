## Independent evidence used for scope

- Khan et al. *Head and Neck Paragangliomas: An Update on the Molecular Classification, State-of-the-Art Imaging, and Management Recommendations.* Radiology: Imaging Cancer. 2022;4(3):e210088. DOI: [10.1148/rycan.210088](https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/10.1148/rycan.210088). Supports large-field-of-view CE-MRA for multifocal lesion detection and SSTR-PET/CT when multifocal or metastatic disease is suspected. It is a specialty review/consensus source, not a primary trial or this lesson's clinical approval.
- Siciliano et al. *Role of Preoperative Embolization in Surgical Management of Carotid Body Tumors: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.* Angiology. 2025;76(1):17–31. DOI: [10.1177/00033197231215240](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11549840/). This internal audit reference found a lower pooled vascular-injury rate but no statistically significant difference in several other outcomes; its mostly retrospective evidence does not establish a universal indication or timing. It is recorded to explain why the PDF treatment bullets were deferred, not to give learners a management recommendation.
