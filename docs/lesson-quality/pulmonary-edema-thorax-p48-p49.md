## Learning path and media

1. Define oedema and explain what PCWP refers to.
2. Read the staged radiographic pattern without treating stages as mandatory or pressure as image-derived.
3. Compare hydrostatic/cardiogenic and permeability patterns probabilistically.
4. Keep the ARDS imaging criterion within its proper diagnostic boundary.
5. Retrieve the distinction using the existing self-check, central MCQs and flashcards.

The three-part SVG is an original code-native explanatory schematic, not a patient study. The PDF pp.48–49 contain no suitable patient image. The lesson's patient examples from PDF p.17 remain labelled as a separate source section.
