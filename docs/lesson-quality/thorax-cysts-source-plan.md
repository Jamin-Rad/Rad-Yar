# Lung cyst differential — source and visual plan

Scope: Thorax.pdf physical p45 names the differential only. Morphology comes from p24–25; the p24 single axial CT (rID10493, extracted original p024-Image367.jpg) is inspected directly and used only to identify a sharply bounded air-containing right-basal space. It does not demonstrate diffuse disease, LAM, BHD or CF. Related PLCH evolution is covered by the dedicated lesson, based on p21.

Sequence: (1) identify the structure, compare cyst/bulla/cavity/honeycombing and bronchial continuity; (2) examine the actual source CT and state the single-image limitation; (3) compare three selected diffuse patterns (PLCH, LAM, BHD) and clinical clues, avoiding an exhaustive disease catalogue. Two transfer questions and two short cards reinforce morphology and diagnostic limits. Primary references: Fleischner 2024 glossary doi:10.1148/radiol.232558; ATS/JRS 2017 LAM guideline PMC5694834; ERN GENTURIS 2024 BHD guideline doi:10.1038/s41431-024-01671-2; adult LCH consensus doi:10.1182/blood.2021014343. All additions beyond PDF explicitly identified in sources. No treatment algorithms or rigid wall-thickness cutoffs.

Visuals: generated conceptual lung hero (never diagnostic); original p24 CT at intrinsic proportions with localized caption; responsive comparison cards rather than a wide table. No invented clinical images. BHD/LAM descriptions are guideline-supported comparison clues, not claims illustrated by the solitary source cyst. DE/EN/FA semantic parity required.
