## PDF page audit

`MSK.pdf` has a physical PDF page number and a printed footer number. Physical p.72 (printed p.71) is a divider only. Physical p.73 (printed p.72) gives aggressive periosteal reactions: onion-skin pattern is classic but not exclusive for Ewing sarcoma or osteomyelitis. Physical p.74 (printed p.73) mentions a solid periosteal reaction in very slowly evolving chronic osteomyelitis, including Brodie abscess; its adjacent radiographs are presented for general periosteal/tumour signs, not identified as infection. Physical p.76 (printed p.75) lists osteomyelitis among possible causes of moth-eaten osteolysis. Physical p.78 (printed p.77) describes a central avascular area with reactive sclerosis in the Brodie-abscess differential for osteoid osteoma. Text extraction and page images for physical pp.72–74, 76 and 78 were inspected. Across those pages no infection teaching case could be independently identified and verified; no septic-arthritis teaching content was found in the audited section.
