## Primary source reconciliation

- European Society of Urogenital Radiology, **ESUR Contrast Media Safety Committee Guidelines 2025**, official [guideline PDF](https://www.esur.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Guidelines-2025-ESUR-vf-1.pdf), renal definitions and haemodialysis pp.14, 18–19. The 2025 document already differentiates macrocyclic from linear GBCA for patients on haemodialysis and says HD has no demonstrated protective effect against PC-AKI or NSF.
- European Society of Urogenital Radiology, **ESUR Contrast Media Safety Committee Guidelines 2026**, official [guideline PDF](https://www.esur.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Guidelines-2026.pdf), CA-AKI definition and dialysis sections pp.14–19. This is the latest posted version consulted; it changes the preferred terminology to CA-AKI and states the updated creatinine threshold/timing.
- American College of Radiology, **Manual on Contrast Media 2026**, official [manual PDF](https://edge.sitecorecloud.io/americancoldf5f-acrorgf92a-productioncb02-3650/media/ACR/Files/Clinical/Contrast-Manual/ACR-Manual-on-Contrast-Media.pdf), CA-AKI pp.39–46 and NSF/dialysis pp.79–84. It describes CA-AKI within 48 h, uses KDIGO criteria, advises no dialysis initiation/schedule change solely for iodinated contrast, and recommends group II GBCA in ESRD. It permits scheduling an elective GBCA MRI before regular HD when feasible but says dialysis should not be initiated or altered solely because of a group II/III agent. Its 2025 update lists linear gadoxetate as ACR group II, so agent structure and NSF-risk group do not map one-to-one; this contributes to the ESUR/ACR dialysis difference.
