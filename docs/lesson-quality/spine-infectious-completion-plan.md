## Source constraints

- Lyme MRI is supportive and may show meningeal/root or cord findings; enhancement does not identify an organism. No sensitivity claim or claim that normal MRI rules disease out.
- Guideline: serum antibodies preferred for suspected neuroborreliosis. If CSF testing is undertaken for suspected CNS involvement, simultaneous CSF/serum antibody index, rather than isolated CSF serology. Index may persist positive; normal index does not securely exclude disease. No treatment prescription.
- Existing-bank source: IDSA adult native vertebral osteomyelitis guideline 2015, doi:10.1093/cid/civ482. TB morphology is suggestive, not proof; paravertebral/epidural location must be distinguished; neural compression and longitudinal extent must be described. This limited supplement does not certify all prior content.
