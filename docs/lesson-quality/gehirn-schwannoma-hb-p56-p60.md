# Vestibular schwannoma / CPA and haemangioblastoma / VHL · Gehirn.pdf pp. 56–60

## Scope and learning path

Source: user-provided `C:/Users/Drham/OneDrive/Desktop/Lerncore/Gehirn.pdf`. PDF pages 56–60 were rendered and visually inspected; the printed folios are one lower (55–59). Exact curricular IDs are `schwannome`, `akustikusneurinom-cpa`, `mesenchymale-tumoren-haemangioblastom`, and `von-hippel-lindau`. There was no dedicated route under `app/gehirn` before this work.

Two routes keep separate image tasks: (1) localise an internal-auditory-canal-centred vestibular schwannoma and compare common CPA mimics; (2) read the cyst/nodule/vascular pattern of CNS haemangioblastoma and recognise when multifocality or systemic context raises a VHL question. Neither route teaches treatment. VHL extra-neural findings are limited to syndrome-recognition cues, not a screening protocol.
