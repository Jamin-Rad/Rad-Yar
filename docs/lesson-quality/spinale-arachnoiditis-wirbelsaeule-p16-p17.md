## Scope and route proposal

- Exact topic ID proposed for coordinator integration: `spinale-arachnoiditis`.
- Proposed route: `/wirbelsaeule/arachnoiditis`.
- Related pre-existing curriculum topic: `meningen-intraspinale-kompartimente` is broader and should not be repointed to this focused lesson.
- Source boundary: physical PDF pp. 16–17 (printed pp. 15–16). Physical p. 18 begins separate headings (epidural abscess, leptomeningitis, etc.); those topics are excluded.
- Learner promise: describe the principal MRI nerve-root patterns attributed to adhesive arachnoiditis and recognize the limits of the classic grouping and its mimics.
- Format: three instructional chapters—context and orientation, root-pattern morphology/MRI features, differential pitfalls—plus one retrieval check and a compact summary. No treatment recommendations.
