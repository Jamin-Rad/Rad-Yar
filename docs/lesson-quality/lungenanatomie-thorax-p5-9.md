## Learning path

1. Locate central structures and septal boundaries in a code-native secondary-lobule schematic.
2. Localise a finding by lobe and bronchopulmonary segment; distinguish lingular S4/S5 from right middle-lobe S4/S5.
3. Explain the combined left basal S7+8 convention and avoid treating segment boundaries as visible CT lines.
4. Relate interstitial compartments to visible patterns without equating a pattern to a diagnosis.
5. Recognize accessory fissures and the azygos fissure/vein configuration as variants, then retrieve these interpretations in short checks.
6. Compress to four portable reporting rules.

## Independent terminology / anatomy references

- Bankier AA, MacMahon H, Colby T, et al. *Fleischner Society: Glossary of Terms for Thoracic Imaging*. Radiology. 2024;310(2):e232558. doi:10.1148/radiol.232558. <https://doi.org/10.1148/radiol.232558>. Used for terminology of acinus, secondary pulmonary lobule and interstitial compartments. It was not used to source the older 1–2.5 cm size range.
- Ohno Y, Seo JB, Parraga G, et al. *Pulmonary Functional Imaging: Part 1—State-of-the-Art Technical and Physiologic Underpinnings*. Radiology. 2021;299(3):508–523. doi:10.1148/radiol.2021203711. <https://doi.org/10.1148/radiol.2021203711>. Used to cross-check approximate secondary lobule size and composition.
- *CT Findings of Azygos Venous System: Congenital Variants and Acquired Structural Changes*. 2024. <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10864146/>. Used to cross-check embryological course and azygos-fissure imaging description.
- *Pulmonary vascular anatomy & anatomical variants*. 2018. <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6039811/>. Used for segmental bronchus/artery and intersegmental-vein relationships and left-sided segment variants.
- Siddharthan S, Xu Z, Spottiswoode B, et al. *A multi-stage 3D convolutional neural network algorithm for CT-based lung segment parcellation*. J Appl Clin Med Phys. 2025. doi:10.1002/acm2.70193. <https://doi.org/10.1002/acm2.70193>. Table 1 supplies segment nomenclature including left anteromedial S7/8 and inferior lingular S5; section 1 states most segment boundaries are not readily visible on clinically acquired CT.
