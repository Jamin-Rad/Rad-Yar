## Learning path

1. Locate central structures and septal boundaries in a code-native secondary-lobule schematic.
2. Relate axial, peripheral and intralobular interstitium to visible patterns without equating a pattern to a diagnosis.
3. Recognize accessory fissures and the segmental boundaries they may mark.
4. Recognize the azygos fissure/vein configuration as a normal variant and retrieve that interpretation in a short check.
5. Compress to three portable reporting rules.
