## Clinical context

An adult HIBI MRI review reports that gyriform contrast enhancement usually appears after 1–2 weeks, peaks after 1–2 months, and commonly resolves after about 6 months: [Hypoxic–Ischemic brain injury in an adult: MRI findings](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4723704/). This supports the broad duration while preserving variability. It is used only to contextualize duration; it does not validate the full HIE lesson.
