## Outline and visual plan (recorded before implementation)

1. **A mantle around vessels:** definition, typical infrarenal/periiliac distribution, medial ureteral deviation; inspect the actual source CT montage.
2. **Follow the ureters:** bilateral obstruction/hydroureteronephrosis and venous compression; identify findings that need prompt clinical communication.
3. **Read tissue, then activity:** CT/MR comparison table and inflammation-to-fibrosis continuum, without a validated imaging stage or ADC cutoff.
4. **Keep causes and mimics open:** idiopathic/IgG4-related/secondary contexts, lymphoma and other mimics; an embedded four-option decision check and locally connected retrieval cards.
5. **Three reporting rules:** compress the pattern, consequences and uncertainty.

## Scientific verification

- Kamper et al. *Diffusion-weighted MRI findings of treated and untreated retroperitoneal fibrosis*. Diagn Interv Radiol. 2014;20:459–463. DOI 10.5152/dir.2014.14027. Original publisher PDF actually opened: https://dirjournal.org/pdf/beb8919b-f013-4ea1-b1c8-40332e840fe1/articles/dir.2014.14027/Diagn%20Interv%20Radiol-20-459-En.pdf . Abstract/results and limitations inspected: treated/untreated DWI differs, overlap exists, quantitative ADC group difference was not significant. Do not teach a universal activity or benignity cutoff.
- Cohan et al. *Imaging appearance of fibrosing diseases of the retroperitoneum: can a definitive diagnosis be made?* Abdom Radiol. 2018;43:1204–1214. DOI 10.1007/s00261-017-1282-5. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28849414/ . Primary abstract retrieved through indexed web result; direct PubMed open was sparse. Characteristic patterns support RPF, but aortic displacement occurs in RPF and absence occurs in cancer. No biopsy algorithm is taught.
- Original comparative CT study: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5358419/ . Indexed methods/results/discussion inspected; direct open challenged. Supports medial ureteral bowing and infrarenal/periiliac distribution as clues, not exclusive tests.
- Original IgG4 cohort: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6481977/ . Indexed full text inspected; direct open challenged. Supports an IgG4-related subset and obstructive consequences; no cohort percentage or laboratory threshold is transferred into the lesson.

Verification date is 2026-10-19; publication dates above are separate. PDF remains the coverage basis. These checks do not constitute subspecialist approval.
