# Osteochondroma · MSK.pdf pp. 84–86

**Status:** bounded independent content review and correction delta PASS; clinical sign-off and browser verification pending.

**Audience and promise:** radiology learners. After this bounded lesson, learners should recognize the defining parent-bone continuity, select the role of MRI for the cartilage cap, and identify features that need further clinical/radiologic assessment.

**Outline:** (1) defining pattern and radiographic example; (2) cartilage-cap assessment and warning signs; (3) surface-lesion differential and complications; (4) one retrieval decision and three take-home rules. No standalone treatment chapter: the PDF’s short management summary is preserved below as a scope note and clinical management is outside this imaging lesson.
