## Source and learning path

Both page images were inspected. The source presents CT staging of colon cancer and MRI staging of rectal cancer, including TNM anatomy, MRF/CRM, EMVI, nodes and metastatic spread. Learning sequence: separate distant staging from local rectal staging; review CT targets; map rectal wall depth; assess MRF/EMVI/nodes; practice structured reporting.

## Assessment and translation

- Lesson self-check: MRF relationship; explanations identify why other isolated measures are insufficient.
- Five focused DE/EN/FA flashcards registered under `kolorektales-karzinom`.
- PDF's older TNM thresholds are intentionally not copied. Verify every threshold against the named current standard before teaching it.
