## Source audit and scope

The supplied `Gehirn.pdf` was rendered at 120 dpi and pages 61–62 were visually inspected on 2026-10-19. Page 61 is a section divider headed “Entzündliche & Demyelinisierende Erkrankungen / Multiple Sklerose (MS)” and contains only the phrase “Dissemination in Zeit und Ort, McDonald-Kriterien.” Page 62 lists NMOSD, MOGAD and ADEM as headings. The pages have no diagnostic MRI, case attribution, criteria table, further imaging findings, references or explanatory prose. Printed footers are 60 and 61 respectively.

The lesson therefore explicitly separates PDF scope from modern external supplements. It teaches learners to compare written pattern descriptions and use a generic anatomical orientation diagram. It does not teach or assess recognition of lesions on real MRI scans. It does not reproduce full diagnostic criteria, treatment, prognosis or a complete inflammatory differential.

## Learning path and media

The lesson moves from an anatomical orientation diagram to a compact comparison table and a written-vignette self-check. The localized code-native SVG shows neutral line diagrams of the brain, optic nerves/chiasm and spinal cord; it has no disease-coded colors, lesions or patient data. It is not diagnostic imaging. No diagnostic image or Radiopaedia case is embedded or claimed. A frame-verified case from an appropriate modality remains a visual requirement for a future revision.

The pattern statements are intentionally non-exclusive: MS ovoid/multiregional lesions; NMOSD optic-pathway, central longitudinal spinal and dorsal medullary/area-postrema clues; variable MOGAD optic-nerve, conus/grey-matter and ADEM-like patterns; and the paediatric ADEM context of acute encephalopathy with multifocal, often larger and poorly marginated lesions. None is presented as a standalone discriminator.
