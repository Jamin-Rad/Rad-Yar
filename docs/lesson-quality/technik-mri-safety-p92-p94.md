## Lesson scope and evidence limits

The existing MRI-artifact block covers image quality, not MRI safety. A concise DE/EN/FA addendum now distinguishes static B₀, RF/SAR heating, and switched gradients/acoustics; localized DE/EN/FA concept visuals, one SAR MCQ and distinct static-field and gradient/acoustics retrieval cards reinforce the hazard-mechanism relationship.
