## Current source verification

- The official [ACR MR Safety page](https://www.acr.org/Clinical-Resources/Clinical-Tools-and-Reference/radiology-safety/mr-safety) identifies the 2026 manual as current. Its official [March 2026 change summary](https://edge.sitecorecloud.io/americancoldf5f-acrorgf92a-productioncb02-3650/media/ACR/Files/Clinical/Radiology-Safety/Changes-to-ACR-Manual-on-MR-Safety.pdf) states that the SAR/B1+rms/specific-energy-dose/specific-absorption section was entirely rewritten to clarify SAR estimation methods. Therefore no legacy table or generic parameter changes are carried forward as universal operational advice.
- The current ACR manual's pregnancy key points (2026 manual, Chapter 15; the March 2026 change summary does not list a pregnancy revision) distinguish patient and personnel: for an indicated pregnant-patient exam, use Normal Operating Mode; pregnant health-care practitioners may work in Zone III/IV except in Zone IV during active scanning. The pregnancy text is available in ACR's official indexing; the full 2026 manual PDF was too large for the browser reader. The official [ACR–SPR Fetal MRI Practice Parameter](https://www.acr.org/-/media/ACR/Files/Practice-Parameters/MR-Fetal.pdf) and ACR manual text define the patient scope: clinical MRI up to 3 T where it is expected to benefit care and no practical alternative supplies the same information; this teaching row explicitly describes non-contrast MRI and does not make a blanket first-trimester prohibition. This recommendation is not presented as a German employment-law determination.
- The official March 2026 ACR change summary also notes possible gradient-induced voltage and implant interaction (including dysfunction, vibration or heating) and says interactions are rare when manufacturer conditions are followed. Its auditory guidance says manufacturer instructions should specify hearing protection and recommends earplugs plus earmuffs whenever possible. The lesson retains a concise, non-numeric statement rather than converting this to a universal intervention for every patient.
- The [BAuA TREMF MR](https://www.baua.de/DE/Angebote/Regelwerk/TREMF/pdf/TREMF-MR.pdf) and [DGUV/IFA EMF rules page](https://www.dguv.de/ifa/fachinfos/elektromagnetische-felder/vorschriften-grenzwerte-und-veroeffentlichungen/index.jsp) support workplace-specific hazard assessment and applicable German occupational controls. They do not justify a universal clinical teaching rule that a pregnant staff member must leave the entire MRI department.
- The ACR manual PDF itself is large and could not be opened by the web reader; source verification used the ACR-published current page and official change summary plus indexed content of the manual's pregnancy section. The pregnant-staff wording is directly corroborated by the ACR manual text indexed in search results. Specialist review remains pending.
