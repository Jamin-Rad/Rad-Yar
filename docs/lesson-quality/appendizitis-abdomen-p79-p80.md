# Appendizitis — Abdomen.pdf pp. 79–80

## Scope and lesson outline

- Route: `/abdomen/gi/appendizitis`; audience: radiology learners.
- Outcome: combine appendix visualisation, direct inflammatory signs, secondary changes, examination limits, population, and clinical context rather than deciding from a single threshold.
- Internal chapter plan (derived from the PDF and learning task):
  1. **Sonographie: Appendix und Umgebung gemeinsam prüfen** — assess the appendix itself, compressibility, wall/layers, vascularity, periappendiceal fat, and the limitation of non-visualisation.
  2. **CT: Entzündung und Komplikationen einordnen** — combine appendiceal calibre and wall with secondary signs and complications; avoid a diameter-only diagnosis.
  3. **Die Modalität an Situation und Population anpassen** — retain the existing cautious modality pathway and make uncertainty explicit.
  4. **Embedded self-check** — use the existing scoped question on a non-visualised appendix; do not create a generic `Befund & Selbsttest` chapter.
  5. **Take-home rules** — retain the shell's concise summary.
- Existing PDF pages are text only; a diagnostic case is supplementary and must be sourced separately.
