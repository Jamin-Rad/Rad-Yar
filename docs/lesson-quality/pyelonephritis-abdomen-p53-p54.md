# Acute pyelonephritis: Abdomen.pdf pages 53–54

Status: limited DE/EN/FA lesson authored; independent review PASS; browser QA and clinical sign-off pending. This source ledger records editorial coverage, not clinical approval.

## Learning promise and outline

Learners should be able to describe focal renal parenchymal hypoenhancement, map emphysematous gas through the perinephric and retroperitoneal spaces, and look for obstruction or fluid collections in clinical context.

1. Identify the parenchymal pattern without treating it as diagnostic by itself.
2. Trace the extent of emphysematous gas and state the PDF's Gerota fascia caveat.
3. Search for abscess and obstruction; keep clinical imaging guidance current and treatment outside scope.
4. Apply the anatomic distinction in an embedded self-check; finish with three portable reporting rules.
