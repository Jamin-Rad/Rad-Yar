## Scope and instructional path

The source page (Gehirn.pdf, PDF p.49 / printed p.48) is a heading page only. It lists pituitary micro/macroadenoma, craniopharyngioma and Rathke cleft cyst without explanatory prose, measured examples or images. This lesson therefore uses the page to define a bounded three-entity differential and supplements it with contemporary external literature. It teaches report-descriptor organization, not image-recognition or definitive diagnosis. It omits treatment, hormone interpretation, epidemiology, WHO grading and a comprehensive sellar/parasellar differential.

Learning path: orient by anatomical centre and adjacent structures; compare location, solid/cystic composition and enhancement; note when dedicated pituitary MRI or CT characterization is relevant; use one embedded self-check to avoid turning a single cyst feature into a diagnosis.

## Sources

- Fleseriu M, et al. **Pituitary incidentaloma: a Pituitary Society international consensus guideline statement**. *Nature Reviews Endocrinology*. 2025;21:638–655. doi:10.1038/s41574-025-01134-8. Supports pituitary-focused imaging and measurement definitions; source scope is incidentaloma management, which is not taught here.
- Park M, et al. **Differentiation between Cystic Pituitary Adenomas and Rathke Cleft Cysts: A Diagnostic Model Using MRI**. *AJNR*. 2015;36:1866–1873. doi:10.3174/ajnr.A4387. Retrospective surgical cohort; used only to support feature-combination framing, not a deterministic classifier.
- Prieto R, Pascual JM, Barrios L. **Topographic Diagnosis of Craniopharyngiomas: The Accuracy of MRI Findings Observed on Conventional T1 and T2 Images**. *AJNR*. 2017;38:2073–2080. doi:10.3174/ajnr.A5361. Retrospective cohort of 200 surgically treated cases; supports anatomic/topographic description and documents limits by category.
- Ugga L, Franca RA, Scaravilli A, Solari D, Cocozza S, Tortora F, Cavallo LM, De Caro MBD, Elefante A. **Neoplasms and tumor-like lesions of the sellar region: imaging findings with correlation to pathology and 2021 WHO classification**. *Neuroradiology*. 2023;65:675–699. doi:10.1007/s00234-023-03120-1. Imaging/pathology review used for qualified CT calcification and differential context.
- User-provided `Gehirn.pdf`, PDF p.49 / printed p.48.
