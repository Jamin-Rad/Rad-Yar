## Current source checks

- [ESUR Guidelines 2025](https://www.esur.org/esur-guidelines-2025/) — current official Contrast Media Safety Committee guideline portal; supports preference for non-ionic iodine-based contrast agents for intravascular administration.
- [ESUR CMSC updated positive GI contrast guidance](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13282205/) — distinguishes enteric ionic high-osmolar agents from non-ionic low-/iso-osmolar products; route and formulation matter.
- [ACR-NASCI-SIR-SPR Body CTA Practice Parameter](https://gravitas.acr.org/PPTS/DownloadPreviewDocument?DocId=164) — non-ionic agent with at least 300 mg I/mL for body CTA; adapt iodine/delivery parameters to scan duration, weight, comorbidities and individual patient.
- [GE Omnipaque product information](https://www.gehealthcare.com/content/dam/gehc/sitecore-migrated-assets/gehc/uk/files/products/contrast-media/ukomppi-09feb2023final--with-2023.pdf) — product strengths and label statement that dose varies by examination, age, weight, cardiac output, patient condition and technique. Local national label remains controlling.
- [ACR-NKF iodinated contrast consensus](https://www.acr.org/-/media/ACR/Files/Clinical-Resources/ACK-NKF-Consensus-Iodinated-Contrast.pdf) — avoid ad hoc dose reduction below a known diagnostic threshold; use the minimum routine clinical diagnostic dose.

These sources verify the teaching principles and challenge the blanket statements; they do not validate the PDF's institution-specific adult volumes or cannula/rate combinations. Those examples remain protocol-dependent and need local specialist review.
