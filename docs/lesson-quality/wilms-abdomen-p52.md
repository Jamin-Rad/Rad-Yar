## Scientific source for the claw sign

Combrink L, Beviss-Challinor KB. *Magnetic resonance imaging for paediatric
retroperitoneal masses: Diagnostic accuracy of the claw sign.* SA Journal of
Radiology. 2021;25(1):2012. doi:10.4102/sajr.v25i1.2012. In 53 children assessed
with MRI, the sign had 97% sensitivity and 74% specificity for renal origin;
the study supports using the sign as a location clue, not as a histology test.
Those MRI-cohort performance estimates are not transferred to CT or to Wilms
diagnosis in the lesson.
