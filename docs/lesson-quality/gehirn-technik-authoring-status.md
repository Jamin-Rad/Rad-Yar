## Technik.pdf

An additive draft route is in `app/technik/grundlagen/page.jsx` with source-backed fundamentals in `data/technikGrundlagenLesson.js`. It currently covers: radiation effects and dose quantities (PDF pp. 2–15); atom, ionization and radiation types (25–27); tube physics, interactions and detector chain (30–43); CT acquisition, reconstruction, dual energy and perfusion (44–58); fluoroscopy/DSA concepts (59–68); protocol choice and an explicitly un-taught RECIST mention (69–75); MRI signal, sequences, artifacts and safety (76–108); flow/diffusion/perfusion/CINE/BOLD (110–119); and ultrasound/Doppler (132–134).

The chapter outline and instructional purpose are: “Wie Strahlung Gewebe schädigen kann” (separate tissue reaction from stochastic risk); “Welche Dosisgröße beantwortet welche Frage?” (choose metric by question); “Optimierung: Fragestellung, Scanbereich und Bildqualität abstimmen” (optimize for diagnostic task); “Was geschieht in der Röntgenröhre?” (connect controls to photon spectrum); “Warum unterscheiden sich Gewebe im Röntgenbild?” (connect attenuation to contrast); “Wie entsteht aus dem Detektorsignal ein Bild?” (compare detector chains); “Von Projektionen zu CT-Schichtbildern” (explain acquisition/reconstruction); “Fluoroskopie und DSA als Bildsequenz verstehen” (explain subtraction and motion); “Protokollphasen an die Fragestellung koppeln” (avoid protocol-by-habit); “MRT-Signal und Kontrastwahl” (link relaxation and sequence); “MRT-Sicherheit und Artefakte richtig einordnen” (recognize safety/artefact limits); “Spezialsequenzen: Fluss, Perfusion und Diffusion” (compare acquisition principles); and “Von Echo zu Doppler in der Sonographie” (connect echo and motion to display).

This is a draft outline, not a set of separate curriculum lessons. It does not yet cover the legal/regulatory material on pp. 16–24 and 28–29, contrast-media pages 120–131 (existing lessons need direct page-by-page comparison), or the blank/unclear page 109. It intentionally excludes legal thresholds, dose values, current screening recommendations, SAR limits and RECIST criteria pending current primary-source review. Pages 74–75 mention RECIST but do not supply sufficient current evidence for teaching the rules. PDF pages 2–15, 25–27, 30–68, 69–75, 76–86, 87–94, 95–108, 110–119 and 132–134 were text-extracted; the visual figures/tables across this whole span have not all been inspected.

DE/EN/FA structure and script parity now pass the local assertion in `scripts/check-technik-grundlagen-parity.mjs`. This only checks required language keys and script contamination; it does not prove translation fidelity. A native/clinical language review is still required.
