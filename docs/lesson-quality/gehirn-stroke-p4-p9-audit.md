## External evidence for clinical selection wording

Prabhakaran S et al. “2026 Guideline for the Early Management of Patients With Acute Ischemic Stroke.” *Stroke*. 2026;57:e316–e436. [AHA Journals DOI 10.1161/STR.0000000000000513](https://doi.org/10.1161/STR.0000000000000513). The guideline treats unknown-onset DWI–FLAIR mismatch and advanced-perfusion selection for 4.5–9 h as distinct imaging-selection pathways. A mismatch is not a precise timestamp; a generic positive CTP is not sufficient.
