# Thumb MCP UCL injury — MSK.pdf pp.22–23

## Teaching plan

**Topic:** ulnar collateral ligament injury of thumb MCP I (skier's thumb), including a Stener lesion. **Route/ID:** `/msk/trauma/ulnares-kollateralband-daumen` / `ulnares-kollateralband-daumen`. **Learner action:** identify the UCL insertion, recognize an avulsion on radiographs, and distinguish a displaced tear from a Stener lesion by its relationship to the adductor aponeurosis. The scope is imaging morphology; clinical stability testing and treatment are outside this lesson.

Chapter path: (1) structure and injury mechanism; (2) radiographic avulsion check and evidence-bounded next imaging; (3) MRI morphology and Stener relationship, illustrated with the supplied PDF case and a focused image question; (4) four portable take-home rules.

## Evidence and wording controls

- ACR Acute Hand and Wrist Trauma says noncontrast hand MRI or hand ultrasound is usually appropriate next in adults with suspected hand tendon/ligament trauma when radiographs show an acute hand fracture, and when MCP/IP malalignment is seen without fracture. The lesson preserves those scenario limits and does not prescribe a universal imaging sequence.
- Qamhawi et al. 2021 pooled diagnostic accuracy for MRI and ultrasound in Stener lesions but noted that all included studies had high risk of bias. The lesson uses the review only to support morphology-based imaging coverage; it imports no sensitivity/specificity figures.
- Melville et al. 2013 describes ultrasound features of displaced UCL tears; no ultrasound sign is presented as infallible.
- No contemporary treatment recommendation is in scope. The supplied PDF's conservative/operative claims and displacement threshold are therefore not carried over.
