- Curriculum ID: `distale-femurfraktur`; intended route `/msk/trauma/distale-femurfraktur`.
- Outcome: distinguish AO 33-A extra-articular, 33-B partial-articular, and 33-C complete-articular morphology; identify a Hoffa fracture as a coronal-plane partial-articular condylar fragment.
- Chapter plan: one focused section moves from the AO distinction to the Hoffa example and retrieval question. A short take-home list reinforces only these points.
