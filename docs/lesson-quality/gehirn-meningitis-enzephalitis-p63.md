## Source boundary and learning objective

`Gehirn.pdf`, PDF page 63 (printed page 62), contains the heading “Infektiöse Erkrankungen” and the subheading “Meningitis/Enzephalitis”. The generic meningitis/encephalitis topic is a heading only. Later on that same page, the PDF presents clinical and MRI material about HIV encephalopathy; that material is excluded from this lesson. PDF p.64 continues the HIV case content. Its image is not a generic meningitis case and was not used.

The supplemental learning objective is introductory: assign described MRI signal changes to meningeal/CSF spaces versus brain parenchyma, then state the limits of that localization. The lesson does not teach pathogen diagnosis, a clinical algorithm, management, or recognition on real diagnostic scans. Its external-source imaging content is clearly identified as supplemental; no imaging finding is attributed to the PDF.
