  [PMID 2643716](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2643716/).

  [NIOSH Silicosis](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/197331).

  [PMID 38337517](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38337517/).
