| CT role: when clinical assessment and radiographs do not confirm sigmoid volvulus, or complication is suspected, CT can clarify diagnosis/complications | Abdomen.pdf physical p.78; updated with current guidance | CT teaching paragraph and linked sources | Qualified wording included in DE/EN/FA. It does not describe CT as the universal first test; WSES 2023 recommends CT when diagnosis is uncertain or ischaemia/perforation is suspected. ASCRS 2021 describes CT as the preferred confirmatory study for sigmoid and caecal volvulus. WSES additionally notes IV contrast may facilitate diagnosis of colonic ischaemia; this contrast-specific point is supplemental source context and intentionally not stated in the lesson. See [WSES recommendation 3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10186802/) and [ASCRS clinical practice guideline](https://fascrs.org/getattachment/Healthcare-Providers/Education/Clinical-Practice-Guidelines/2021-Colonic-Volvulus-CPG.pdf?lang=en-US). |
