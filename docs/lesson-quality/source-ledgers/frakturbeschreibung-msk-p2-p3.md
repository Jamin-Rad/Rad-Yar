## Instructional and assessment check

The existing lesson turns source terms into a reporting sequence and adds displacement, joint involvement, soft-tissue status and imaging beyond pp.2–3. Those are retained as supplemental curriculum, not mislabeled as content from these pages. The source distinction among fatigue/insufficiency/pathological mechanisms appears in the lesson table, 12 questions (DE/EN/FA) and 15 cards. The four source groups on morphology appear in the lesson table; retrieval questions/cards cover torsion, segmental and avulsion patterns. No p.2–3-only case image is available or needed; the source pages are text-only.

The underlying source figure visible later on PDF p.4 is displayed separately in the route with an attribution. It is not used to claim p.2–3 coverage. Browser rendering of the route was not part of this source audit.
