# Source and design ledger — `gefaesse-pavk-stenosen`

**Route:** `/msk/gefaesse/pavk-stenosen`

**Audience / outcome:** Radiology learners; describe the clinical presentation, imaging roles and lesion morphology of lower-extremity PAD without treating a source table as an individual management algorithm.

**Learning goals:** Map Fontaine stages to presentation; distinguish duplex/CTA/MRA/DSA roles; describe lesion morphology and qualify older TASC and walking-distance treatment mappings.

**Scope:** Educational imaging and morphology summary, not individual diagnosis or treatment advice.

## Chapter outline and visual plan

1. **Which clinical presentation?** Learn the Fontaine symptom spectrum; distinguish classification from current CLTI assessment. Visual: labelled leg-artery schematic in hero; no patient data.
2. **Which imaging answers which question?** Compare duplex, CTA, MRA, and DSA, including source-specific limitations. Visual: compact semantic table.
3. **How does morphology guide description?** Separate lesion descriptors and procedural concepts from management decisions; qualify TASC. Visual: comparison table and explanatory note.
4. **Read the source critically**: one retrieval question checks the 200 m and TASC caveats with explanatory feedback.
5. **Take-home rules**: compress to three portable rules.
