## Supplemental evidence, distinct from PDF coverage

| Claim | Evidence / disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Relative CCD thresholds for Rockwood II, III and V | Commonly used thresholds are supported by a recent prospective cohort and radiographic classification review: [prospective cohort](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10557934/) and [inter-/intrarater classification study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12081960/). Type II <25%, Type III 25–100%, and Type V >100% (often 100–300%) compare the injured side with the uninjured side. Do not use PDF’s absolute 25 mm as a defining boundary. |
| Normal CCD 11–13 mm | Not given as independent evidence on p.12; corroborated by [AJR imaging review](https://doi.org/10.2214/AJR.13.11460), which also notes measurement technique and side-to-side assessment. Individual variance is retained in the lesson. |
| Axial/axillary view for suspected posterior displacement | [AJR imaging review](https://doi.org/10.2214/AJR.13.11460) and [radiology review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9940602/) support its value in assessing posterior displacement. This does not establish that every patient requires an axial view. |
| Treatment principles | [ESA–ESSKA Delphi consensus](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00167-020-06286-w) states conservative care is usually preferred for I–II, symptomatic IV–VI are routinely managed surgically, and acute III remains controversial; it also notes decision factors and lack of a surgical gold standard. A [2023 evidence review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9940602/) notes insufficient high-level evidence overall. These are supplemental to the PDF’s brief treatment outline. |
| Weighted/stress projections | [Nordin et al.](https://doi.org/10.1177/0284185120939270), [Ibrahim et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.injury.2015.06.028), and the [AJR review](https://doi.org/10.2214/AJR.13.11460) describe differing evidence and lower load examples than the PDF. Keep the 10–15 kg per-side value as a PDF-attributed statement only; protocol-specific clinical review remains appropriate. |
| Other lesson material | Epidemiology, indirect mechanism, rehabilitation timing, device details, and any claims outside p.12–13 are not made PDF coverage by this ledger. They remain outside this bounded source pass unless listed above. |
