# Small-bowel feces sign — Abdomen.pdf p.71

Scope: a source-bounded addition to the existing `/abdomen/gi/ileus-volvulus` lesson under its existing curriculum ID `ileus-duenndarm`. The source slide is physical Abdomen.pdf p.71 / printed p.70; the rendered source page was inspected directly. No new lesson route or curriculum topic was created.

| Source point | Lesson disposition | Evidence and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The slide defines the sign as stool-like material in small bowel immediately before an obstruction and associates it with subacute/chronic course | Added as a concise definition and a schematic showing dilated bowel, particulate/gas content near a transition, and collapsed distal bowel | The slide contains no labelled CT example of this sign. The added diagram is explicitly identified as schematic and not a CT image. |
| The slide also lists small-bowel dilatation and the transition zone | The existing lesson already teaches dilatation and transition; retained in its current obstruction content rather than duplicated | PDF slide threshold (>2.5–3 cm) was not added as a universal threshold because this bounded gap is about the sign and the source gives a range rather than a single harmonised criterion. |
| The sign may help locate the transition zone, but does not independently determine severity | Added a limitation note and a targeted MCQ/flashcards | Mayo-Smith et al. described particulate matter mixed with gas in dilated small bowel; most patients in their small retrospective series had SBO, but the sign also occurred with other small-bowel abnormalities (PMID 7489626). A later retrospective adhesive-SBO cohort found that the sign helped identify the transition zone but was not independently associated with ischaemia or successful non-operative treatment (PMID 29045179). No treatment recommendation is inferred. |
