The lesson covers these selected signs and does not claim to reproduce every possible imaging feature of pulmonary hypertension. It contains no source patient case. An original code-native schematic is clearly labeled non-diagnostic and contains no patient data.

## Learning design and media

Objective: distinguish imaging clues from haemodynamic diagnosis; recognise selected radiographic and CT signs; relate findings to the current five-group mechanism framework without assigning an aetiology from one sign; complete a short decision check.

Sequence: haemodynamic definition and catheterisation; radiograph and CT clues with the schematic; five groups; immediate-feedback check; three reporting rules. No separate default chapter named “Befund & Selbsttest” was added.

## Scientific reconciliation

- ESC/ERS 2022 defines PH by mPAP >20 mmHg at rest. Pre-capillary PH additionally requires PAWP ≤15 mmHg and PVR >2 WU. The lesson does not call mPAP alone PAH or use it to assign a group.
- ESC/ERS CT clues include enlarged PA, PA/Ao >0.9 and right-heart enlargement. These are supportive signs; none confirms PH alone.
- The current group-4 label is “PH associated with pulmonary artery obstructions”; CTEPH is presented as the principal group 4.1 example. Group-4 imaging clues include webs/bands, stenoses or cut-off, mosaic perfusion and bronchial collaterals.
- The PDF thresholds (main PA >29 mm; PA/Aorta >1.0) remain attributed to the PDF and are explicitly not promoted to universal diagnostic criteria.
- CXR/CT signs and haemodynamic definitions were checked against the [2022 ESC/ERS guideline](https://academic.oup.com/eurheartj/article/43/38/3618/6673929). The threshold definitions were cross-checked against [Kovacs et al., ERS 2024](https://doi.org/10.1183/13993003.01324-2024). This author check is not subspecialist approval.
