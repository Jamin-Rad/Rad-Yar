## Chapter and visual plan

1. **Where does the fracture line run?** Learn the 31A trochanteric patterns, medial versus lateral neck location, and the qualified capsule relationship. The localized, language-neutral diagram marks six anatomical regions only; the text and HTML table separately describe the pertrochanteric and reverse-obliquity directions.
2. **What do Garden and Pauwels describe?** Garden summarizes completeness/displacement; Pauwels describes line inclination. Include a non-diagnostic angle schematic and four-stage Garden schematic, all labels localized in the surrounding DE/EN/FA table. An inline retrieval check tests that Garden displacement does not directly measure perfusion.

## External evidence used for corrections and scope

- AO Foundation, [Impacted or nondisplaced subcapital femoral neck fractures](https://surgeryreference.aofoundation.org/orthopedic-trauma/adult-trauma/proximal-femur/femoral-neck-fracture-subcapital-impacted-or-nondisplaced/definition): Pauwels angle thresholds `<30°`, `30°–50°`, `>50°`.
- AO Foundation, [Basicervical femoral neck fractures](https://surgeryreference.aofoundation.org/orthopedic-trauma/adult-trauma/proximal-femur/femoral-neck-fracture-basicervical/definition): extra-articular but potentially intracapsular; reinforces the need to qualify a blanket extracapsular label.
- Wang et al., [An update on the Pauwels classification](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13018-016-0498-3): identifies the 30°/70° thresholds as a common misinterpretation and discusses limitations.
- Konarski et al., [The Risk of Avascular Necrosis Following the Stabilization of Femoral Neck Fractures](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9408780/): pooled displaced versus undisplaced association; no significant difference by individual Garden stage in the reviewed data, with limitations.
- van Embden et al., [Femoral neck fracture: the reliability of radiologic classifications](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8787877/): classification reliability limits.
- AO Foundation, [Simple two-part pertrochanteric fractures](https://surgeryreference.aofoundation.org/orthopedic-trauma/adult-trauma/proximal-femur/trochanteric-fracture-simple-pertrochanteric-two-part/definition) and [Intertrochanteric fractures](https://surgeryreference.aofoundation.org/orthopedic-trauma/adult-trauma/proximal-femur/trochanteric-fracture-intertrochanteric/definition): anatomical pattern terminology.
- AO describes Pauwels angle as best determined intraoperatively after traction and reduction; the lesson schematic is explicitly a learning aid, not a definitive grading method.
