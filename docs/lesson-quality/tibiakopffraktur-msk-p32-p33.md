- Extracted text and rendered full-page images of physical pp.32–33 were inspected. p.32 contains a six-type anatomical teaching schematic (Image375.jpg), not a patient image. p.33 contains the Segond/reverse-Segond text and an AP knee radiograph; the embedded Image382.jpg was inspected separately and maps to the lateral plateau fragment shown on that page.

- Reverse-Segond caveat is supported by Peltola et al. (2014), a retrospective study of 1,553 CT examinations, PMID 24395399, and the earlier small case series Escobedo et al. (2002), PMID 11906886. These establish reported associations, not a deterministic injury combination. Segond association percentages from the PDF are not carried over.
- Schatzker morphology follows the supplied diagram/text and Kfuri & Schatzker (2018), PMID 30526924. The PDF's `<4 mm` figure is not presented as a universal current threshold. No treatment, weight-bearing, prognosis, or modality decision protocol is taught.
- Retrieval question tests type II; answer and option-specific feedback are parallel across German, English and Persian.
