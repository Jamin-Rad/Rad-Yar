## PDF statements not adopted as universal current requirements

| PDF statement | Why not reproduced as a general rule | Replacement in the lesson |
| --- | --- | --- |
| StrlSchG “sets the Directive” and every exposure is “medically justified” | Does not distinguish federal Act and Ordinance, justification of activity types (§ 6) and an individual medical indication (§ 83). | Explain both legal decisions separately and link the relevant law. |
| SSV is usually hospital management and needs no expertise except as sole operator | Too categorical; legal responsibility follows the authorization/notification structure and, for organizations, the authorized representative. SSB appointment and expertise depend on task and need. | Describe the statutory role and written assignment of SSB tasks and authority; retain SSV responsibility. |
| Area classification is only based on effective dose; only “staff and patients” may enter a control area | Incomplete: § 52 includes organ and local-skin dose criteria; § 53 and § 55 contain additional marking/access conditions. | Teach the prospective area-classification principle and point readers to current statutes for criteria and exceptions. |
| A/B categories, periodic health checks, badge requirements and multiple dose thresholds as one fixed checklist | Specific requirements depend on the person, potential exposure, task and applicable current provisions. A quick legacy summary is insufficient evidence for a complete operational checklist. | Omit the checklist and numeric limits in this focused lesson. |
| Every doctor who orders and is responsible needs one undifferentiated form of Fachkunde; full pathway takes 36 months and CT partial qualification 12 months | Qualification depends on application, individual tasks and current recognition pathways; statutory framework provides the general criteria, not a universal time promise. | Explain Fachkunde versus knowledge, application-specific qualification, recognition and the individual indication. |
| Only MTR may perform technical acquisition; MFA are categorically prohibited in teleradiology | Current § 14(2) and § 145 set expertise and authorization requirements and enumerate multiple eligible groups/conditions. An individual's eligibility must be determined under the exact applicable role and rules. | Describe required expertise and legal eligibility; do not make a blanket occupational exclusion. |
| Teleradiologist must arrive within 45 minutes and visit the site annually | § 14(2) federal text specifies presence within the time required for emergency care and regular close integration, without those fixed 45-minute/annual intervals. More specific local rules may apply. | State the wording of § 14(2); direct users to competent authority and local license for operating detail. |
| Five-year permit term stated without identifying which authorization | Independent review noted that § 14(2), sentence 4, limits only the exception authorization under sentence 3 for operating outside night/weekend/holiday service. | Corrected DE/EN/FA and independently retested against the official current statute; finding closed. |
