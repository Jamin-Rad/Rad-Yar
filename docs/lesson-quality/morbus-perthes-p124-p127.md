## Learning path and scope

Learner task: recognize the clinical context, read the time course on radiography, know when MRI can show earlier change, and keep two named differential groups in view. The sequence is clinical pattern → temporal imaging model → modality roles → differential → four retrieval rules.

Treatment and individual prognosis are deferred. The PDF’s containment options, operative choices and age-based prognosis were not independently verified for this bounded imaging lesson, and are not repeated. The PDF’s predominantly unilateral statement is retained; its bilateral percentage is omitted because the denominator is unclear. Age and sex-ratio statements remain explicitly attributed to the PDF.
