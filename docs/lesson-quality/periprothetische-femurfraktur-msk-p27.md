# Periprothetische Femurfrakturen (Vancouver) — MSK.pdf p.27

## Scope and learning path

Route: `/msk/trauma/periprothetische-femurfraktur` · curriculum ID: `periprothetische-femurfraktur`.

Audience: radiology learners reviewing hip arthroplasty imaging. Learners should be able to classify the fracture location and distinguish B1/B2/B3 by stem fixation and remaining bone stock, while recognizing that a real stability assessment may need prior imaging, implant history and orthopaedic input.

Sections: (1) locate AG/AL, B and C relative to the stem; (2) compare B1/B2/B3 and cautiously contextualize the PDF's management associations; (3) retrieve B2 from a short case. Visuals: bespoke non-diagnostic SVG orientation schematic plus the supplied PDF's specimen comparison image.

## Independent clinical sources

- AO Foundation, “The new VA-LCP Periprosthetic Proximal Femur Plating System,” sections “Fracture classification” and “Complications”: https://www.aofoundation.org/approved/approvedsolutionsfolder/2021/ppfx-femur-plating-system (accessed 2026-10-20). Confirms Vancouver classification criteria and describes management as typical rather than absolute, including selected alternatives.
- Di Martino A, Brunello M, Villari E, et al. Stem revision vs. internal fixation in Vancouver B2/B3 periprosthetic hip fractures: systematic review and meta-analysis. *Arch Orthop Trauma Surg*. 2024;144:3787–3796. doi:10.1007/s00402-024-05469-1. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00402-024-05469-1. The review reports both options in contemporary studies and does not support presenting the PDF's treatment mapping as an inflexible patient-level rule.
