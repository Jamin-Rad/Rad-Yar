# Gehirn.pdf completion ledger — 2026-10-23

## Scope and evidence

This ledger tracks only `Gehirn.pdf` (89 physical PDF pages; German source) against the current RadYar Main snapshot `5a2a54fd`. The source inventory covered all 89 rendered pages and extracted text; page images were visually surveyed in contact sheets, with selected tables, panels, and ambiguous pages inspected at larger scale. This is a topic-to-route completion map, not a fresh page-by-page clinical or lesson-quality audit. Page numbers below are physical PDF pages; printed folios differ in the dementia pages (PDF pp.72–74 are printed pp.71–73) and in other identified legacy pages.

Status terms: **Mapped** means a relevant route teaches or dispositioned the source topic within a documented bounded scope. It does not mean the entire route is complete or clinically approved. **Supplemental** means the PDF offers only a heading/name or insufficient teaching detail and the lesson needs explicitly sourced material beyond the PDF. **Pending authoring** means a substantive topic still lacks its dedicated route/lesson. **Later visual optimization** means a route exists, while real-image recognition practice is intentionally a later enhancement.
