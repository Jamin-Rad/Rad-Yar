# NPH lesson — Gehirn.pdf pp.69–71 source addendum

Physical PDF pp.69–71 (printed pp.68–70) were visually compared with the
existing `/gehirn/anatomie/normaldruckhydrozephalus` lesson. The route already
covers the central ventriculomegaly/DESH pattern, measurements, supportive MRI
signs, differential and cases. This is a bounded source addendum, not a new
lesson or a claim of complete Gehirn.pdf coverage.

| PDF point | Disposition | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Lateral and third ventricular enlargement with a relatively normal fourth ventricle | Added under morphology as a possible configuration, with a warning to assess the aqueduct/outflow and not use it alone to diagnose or exclude NPH/obstruction | The PDF observation is compatible with hydrocephalus anatomy, but is not taught as a validated independent NPH sign. Kiefer & Unterberg discuss it as a descriptive pattern and emphasize the broader clinical/imaging context. |
| Caudal hypothalamic displacement | Omitted from the core NPH lesson | The supporting literature found concerns general/marked hydrocephalus rather than an iNPH-specific marker; adding it here could imply unsupported specificity. |
| Intraventricular lactate on MRS | Omitted from the routine diagnostic pathway | Small early studies and partial-volume/source uncertainty; the Japanese iNPH guideline describes diagnostic value as unclear, and later reviews call it controversial. |
| Generalized glucose hypometabolism on PET | Omitted as an NPH hallmark | PET findings are heterogeneous and nonspecific; PET is not routine structural imaging or a stand-alone shunt-selection test. |
