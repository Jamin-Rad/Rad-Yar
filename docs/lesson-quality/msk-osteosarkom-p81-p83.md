## Learning path

1. Define the tumour by direct osteoid production and place the source-reported typical age/location pattern in context.
2. Read matrix, destruction, periosteal reaction and soft-tissue component as a combined aggressive pattern, not a standalone diagnosis.
3. Separate radiograph/CT assessment of matrix and cortex from MRI mapping of local extent and skip lesions.
4. Use source-listed differentials as prompts for further evaluation, then retrieve the next imaging action.
