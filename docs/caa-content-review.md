Reviewed on 2026-10-13 against the supplied older text and three screenshots. Changes apply to the reading lesson (`data/caaLesson.js`), preserving the reference layout, independent Take Home component, navigation and progress. The separate quiz/flashcard bank is outside this review.

## Coverage

- **Foundations:** vessel-wall β-amyloid in cortical/leptomeningeal arteries and arterioles; wall damage and loss of elasticity; distinction from systemic amyloidosis. Restore age, hereditary Dutch type, Alzheimer and Down syndrome associations.
- **MRI markers:** four bleeding patterns with location, sequence, appearance and clinical relevance, followed by the two white-matter markers.
- **Clinical manifestations:** a separate chapter between MRI and Boston, containing acute lobar ICH, TFNE and its teaching note, cognitive decline, seizures and headache. Keep cognitive symptoms distinct from mandatory diagnostic criteria. The desktop and mobile learning paths, continue action and progress denominator derive from the same five tracked chapter IDs; saved valid read IDs remain preserved.
- **Boston:** explicitly explain clinical–radiological classification without biopsy, age ≥50, qualifying presentation and exclusions; preserve the v2.0 lesion-count and white-matter rules and the neutral treatment of cerebellar bleeding.
- **Differentials:** restore explicit CAA, hypertensive arteriolosclerosis, AVM, cavernoma and haemorrhagic tumour comparison, with RCVS, venous thrombosis and CAA-related inflammation as additional work-up clues.

## Material corrections to the supplied text

| Supplied statement | Decision and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| CAA causes 15–20% of all ICH in patients >60 | Do not present as a universal attributable fraction. Age, cohort, diagnostic approach and pathology thresholds change estimates. Pathological CAA prevalence among patients with ICH does not itself establish that CAA caused each bleed. Teach CAA as a leading cause of spontaneous lobar ICH in older adults. [Jäkel et al., 2022](https://alz-journals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/alz.12366). |
| CAA in 85% of Alzheimer cases | Replace with the pooled estimate of approximately 79% for **mild-to-severe histological CAA** in AD. Moderate-to-severe thresholds yield different estimates. Preserve the distinction between vascular amyloid and parenchymal plaques and do not invert the association. [Jäkel et al., 2022](https://alz-journals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/alz.12366). |
| M = F | State both sexes are affected and sex is not a Boston criterion. Exact equality is unnecessary and not supported across heterogeneous cohorts; differences in onset/course have been reported. [Koemans et al., 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9855754/). |
| Dementia in 30% | Do not give a universal percentage without a defined cohort. Explain cognitive impairment, mixed vascular/AD contribution and its role as one possible qualifying Boston presentation, not a mandatory symptom. [Imaging review, 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11592298/), [Boston v2.0](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9389452/). |
| TFNE from cortical irritation instead of embolus | Teach recurrent stereotyped, often spreading symptoms with cSAH/cSS. Identify cortical spreading depolarisation as a **proposed** mechanism; do not call all TFNE epileptic or infer embolism. [Smith et al., 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34016709/). |
| Headache proves recurrent microbleeds; seizures are chronic manifestations | Headache is nonspecific and may accompany acute bleeding. Seizures can occur acutely or as a sequela of cortical injury. [Imaging review, 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11592298/). |
| All patterns strictly lobar/cortical | Lobar ICH/CMB may be cortical **or subcortical**. cSAH/cSS follow superficial sulci. Deep ICH/CMB preclude MRI probable/possible Boston categories but do not biologically rule out coexisting CAA. [Boston v2.0](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9389452/). |
| Cavernoma: no oedema, no enhancement | Correct: enhancement can occur and recent haemorrhage can produce surrounding oedema. Preserve popcorn appearance/haemosiderin rim; small cavernomas may mimic CMB. [Akers et al., consensus recommendations, 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12680281/). |

## Layout decisions

The screenshots are evidence of missing teaching content, not instructions to reproduce their dense cards or absolute claims. Use short semantic paragraphs, simple definition lists and three-column tables in German, English and Persian. Separate imaging appearance from clinical significance within each table cell. Mobile tables scroll within their labelled region; they do not overflow the page. Keep the existing topic-specific hero, restrained handwritten labels and shared Test Take Home layout. Do not restore the removed Befund & Selbsttest chapter.
